# DOB Reference Library, Index

Wiki-style index of primary-source code documents in this folder. Every file is fetched directly from nyc.gov (DOB, DCP). One entry per document: what it is, the sections we have actually been cited on, and which objection brought it into the library.

Rule for adding entries: primary source only (nyc.gov), never UpCodes, ICC model text, or code-summary sites. Note the code year or version on every entry. Add the objection that triggered the fetch so the library doubles as an objection playbook.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Administration and Filing

### AC_Title28_Ch1_Administration_2014CC.pdf
NYC Administrative Code Title 28, Chapter 1 (Administration), 2014 Construction Codes print with amendment notes.
Key sections: 28-104 (construction documents), 28-104.7 (plans, incl. 28-104.7.6 datum / NAVD 88), 28-118.4 (final lot survey filed as amendment before completion).
Cited on: Powers B01433167-I1 objections 08/13/2026 and 08/18/2026 (Teolinda Gross), nearly every item carries "per AC 28-104" or "AC 28-104.7".
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/cons_codes_2014/2014CC_AC_Chapter1_Administration.pdf

### BC_Ch1_Administration_2014CC.pdf
NYC Building Code Chapter 1, 2014 code.
Key section: BC 107.3 (lot diagram): "drawn in accordance with an accurate boundary survey to the city datum", established grade and curb elevations, proposed final grades.
Cited on: Powers objection 3 (site survey) and the 08/18 conform-to-survey objection.
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/cons_codes_2014/2014CC_BC_Chapter_1_Administration.pdf

### BC_Ch3_Occupancy_Classification_2014CC.pdf
NYC Building Code Chapter 3 (Use and Occupancy Classification), 2014 code.
Key sections: BC 310 (Residential Group R, R-3 at 1-2 dwelling units), BC 312 (Group U, accessory storage buildings).
Cited on: Powers objection 1 (plot plan occupancy classification, existing and proposed).
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/cons_codes_2014/2014CC_BC_Chapter_3_Use_and_Occupancy_Classification.pdf

## Zoning Resolution (current text, DCP)

### ZR_Art-I_Ch2_Definitions_12-10.pdf
ZR Article I Chapter 2, all definitions.
Key entries: floor area ("measured from the exterior faces of exterior walls or from the center lines of walls separating two buildings", the party wall measurement rule), cellar, basement, curb level, accessory use, building.
Cited on: Powers floor area breakdown objections (08/13 item 5, 08/18 dimension comment), cellar deduction, survey reconciliation.
Source: zr.planning.nyc.gov, Article I Chapter 2 chapter PDF

### ZR_Art-II_Ch3_Residential_Bulk_23-xx.pdf
ZR Article II Chapter 3, residential district bulk regulations.
Key sections: 23-12 (lot area and width), 23-22 (FAR, R6 through R12), 23-311 / 23-312 (rear yard equivalents cited by the examiner), 23-322 (front yards R6-R12), 23-342 (rear yards), 23-432 (height and setback, base plane in contextual districts).
Cited on: Powers objection 6 (rear yard building) and the 08/18 zoning analysis coordination objection (height, rear yard, base plane).
Source: zr.planning.nyc.gov, Article II Chapter 3 chapter PDF

### ZR_Art-II_Ch5_Accessory_Parking_25-xx.pdf
ZR Article II Chapter 5, accessory off-street parking.
Key section: 25-21 (required parking, Inner Transit Zone, zero requirement post Dec 2024 units).
Cited on: A-002 zoning table row, not yet objected, kept for completeness.
Source: zr.planning.nyc.gov, Article II Chapter 5 chapter PDF

### ZR_Art-V_Ch4_NonComplying_Bldgs_54-xx.pdf
ZR Article V Chapter 4, non-complying buildings.
Key section: 54-11 (continuation of non-compliance).
Cited on: Powers objection 6 answer, the 1932 rear storage building continues as legal non-complying under 54-11 rather than being tested against 23-311 / 23-312.
Source: zr.planning.nyc.gov, Article V Chapter 4 chapter PDF

## Bulletins and Rules

### BB_2012-008_Cellar_Accessory_Use.pdf
Buildings Bulletin 2012-008 (Zoning, June 29 2012), permitted size of residential cellar used for non-dwelling purposes.
Key content: condition (a), cellar perimeter walls within perimeter walls of the story above, or condition (b) with the 50 percent test.
Cited on: A-003 cellar accessory use note, Powers cellar deduction.
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/bldgs_bulletins/bb_2012-008.pdf

### 1_RCNY_5000-01.pdf
Rule for energy code filings: Figure 1 Energy Analysis, Figure 2 Supporting Documentation Index, Table I Progress Inspections. 2020 ECC edition, still in force; 2025 edition listed as coming soon.
Cited on: Apollo EN-a.9 / EN-a.10 (07/23/2026, examiner ChunNg), Powers EN sheet conformance.
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/rules/1_RCNY_5000-01_2020ECC.pdf

## Energy Code (2025 NYCECC)

### NYCECC_2025_Ch4RE_Residential_Energy.pdf
2025 NYCECC residential Chapter 4 [RE], Residential Energy Efficiency.
Key sections: Table R402.1.2 and R402.3 (fenestration and skylight U-factors, CZ4 skylight max U-0.50), R403.5.2 (pipe insulation, 1 inch min with conductivity band, not R-3), R403.7, R404.
Cited on: Apollo EN-a.5 / EN-r.2 (skylight U), Apollo EN-a.9 / EN-a.10 resolution (Detail E citation fix).
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_r4.pdf

### NYCECC_2025_Ch5RE_Existing_Buildings.pdf
2025 NYCECC residential Chapter 5 [RE], Existing Buildings (alterations, repairs, additions, change of occupancy).
Key sections: R501.1 scope, R503 alterations (the basis for the unaltered-portions exemption cited on A-001).
Cited on: both jobs are alterations, governs which envelope provisions apply.
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/codes-pdf/energy_code_2025/2025nycecc_r5.pdf

### DOBNOW_2020_NYCECC_Objections_List.pdf
The DOB NOW energy objection catalogue, the actual EN-a.x / EN-l.x / EN-r.x codes examiners issue. 2020 ECC edition; 2025 listed as coming soon.
Cited on: Apollo EN-a.9, EN-a.10, EN-a.5, EN-r.2. Read the entry before answering any EN objection, it states exactly what the examiner is looking for.
Source: nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/DOBNOW_2020_NYCECC_Objections_List.pdf
